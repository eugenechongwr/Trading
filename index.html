Trade rules (Chart & Entry Spec v6.0)
  1. Trigger: a 30-min bar closes above the 30-min 21 EMA. Enter at the next 30-min bar's open. Nothing else fires an entry.
  2. Initial stop is the 30-min pullback low, never wider than half the stock's ADR. Hard stop at fill — no mental stops.
  3. First third off at 2R, or at T+3 if 2R hasn't come. Stop to breakeven on the rest.
  4. Runner trails the daily 9 EMA on a closing basis.
  5. Grade sets size. Under the 200 SMA but above the 50: one grade down. Under the 50 SMA: no long setup.
